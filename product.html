Storage Engine

Enterprise Storage That Thinks

Not just a hard drive with a network port. SphereX understands what's stored on it — and makes it searchable, organized, and intelligent.

🔍

Semantic File Search

Search by describing what you're looking for, not the filename. SphereX reads the content of your documents, PDFs, spreadsheets, and even images — and returns results ranked by relevance.

  • Content-aware search across all file types
  • Vector-indexed for sub-second retrieval across terabytes
  • "Find the invoice from the electrician in March" works instantly
  • No other consumer or prosumer NAS offers this capability
🔄

Multi-Device Auto Sync

Every photo taken, every document saved — automatically backed up to SphereX over your local network. Works with iPhone, Android, Mac, and Windows. No cloud uploads, no storage limits, no monthly fees.

  • Continuous background sync over WiFi or LAN
  • Apple Time Machine and Windows File History compatible
  • Version history and incremental snapshots for every file
  • Replaces iCloud, Google One, and Dropbox entirely
🌐

Secure Remote Access

Access your files from anywhere — at the office, on the road, or across the world. Encrypted end-to-end with direct tunneling to your SphereX. No cloud relay service, no third-party intermediary.

  • Zero-knowledge encrypted tunnel to your home or office device
  • Mobile and desktop apps with seamless file browsing
  • Share files with temporary secure links
  • Works on any network without port forwarding
🔐

Hardware-Accelerated Encryption

AES-256 encryption at rest and in transit, executed on dedicated hardware. Immutable snapshots protect against ransomware. Zero outbound telemetry — your SphereX never phones home.

  • Full-disk AES-256 encryption with hardware acceleration
  • Immutable snapshot protection against ransomware attacks
  • Zero telemetry — no analytics, no usage data collected
  • Air-gap capable for high-security environments
