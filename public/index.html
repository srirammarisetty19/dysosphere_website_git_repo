Every organization faces the same question: trust someone else with your data, or own your intelligence stack.

🛡️

Absolute Data Sovereignty

Your data is physically on your premises. Not in a data center you don't control, not subject to foreign jurisdiction, not accessible to any third party. Full compliance with data residency requirements.

💰

Zero Recurring Cost

No per-seat SaaS fees. No per-token API charges. No cloud storage subscriptions. Buy once, deploy forever. The total cost of ownership drops dramatically compared to cloud AI + cloud storage stacks.

🏗️

Hardware You Own

A physical appliance with GPU/NPU acceleration, enterprise-grade storage, and multi-gigabit networking. Not a VM on someone else's rack — a machine that sits in your office and answers to you.

📜

Regulatory Compliance

Built for industries where data leaving the premises is not an option. HIPAA, GDPR, SOX, FERPA — SphereX enables AI adoption without triggering compliance violations.

Air-Gap Capable

SphereX operates entirely offline when required. No internet dependency for AI inference, storage, or internal search. Critical for defense, government, and high-security environments.

Unified AI + Storage

No integration headaches. The AI engine and the storage layer are one system. Your LLMs read directly from your NAS — no ETL pipelines, no data duplication, no middleware.
