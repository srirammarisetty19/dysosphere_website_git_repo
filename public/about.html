About DysoSphere

We Believe Your Intelligence
Should Belong to You

DysoSphere is building the future of private, local AI — starting with SphereX.

Our Mission

Democratizing AI by Making It Local

The promise of AI was empowerment — that intelligent machines would work for you. Instead, the AI revolution has become a data extraction engine. Every conversation, every file, every query feeds someone else's model, trained on your data, sold back to you as a subscription.

We believe this is fundamentally broken.

DysoSphere was founded on a radical premise: your AI should run on your hardware, process your data locally, and answer only to you. No cloud. No subscriptions. No compromise.

SphereX is the first product of that vision — a device that sits on your shelf and gives you the power of a personal AI assistant and enterprise-grade storage, all in one box, all under your control.

The Problem We're Solving

Why Cloud AI Is Broken

🕳️

Privacy Erosion

Every cloud AI conversation trains someone else's model. Your personal data — conversations, files, preferences — becomes product. Cloud providers monetize your intelligence while charging you for access.

💸

Subscription Fatigue

$20/month for ChatGPT. $10/month for cloud storage. $5/month for calendar AI. The costs compound forever. You never own anything — you rent access to your own data and intelligence.

🔒

Vendor Lock-In

Your photos are on Google. Your files are on Dropbox. Your AI history is on OpenAI. Switching is designed to be painful. Your data is held hostage by the platforms that store it.

🌍

Data Sovereignty

Your data lives in data centers across jurisdictions you don't control. Subject to foreign regulations, government subpoenas, and corporate policy changes. You have no physical control over your own information.
